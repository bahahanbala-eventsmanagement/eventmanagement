Baha Hanbala, Exhibition & Museum Project Consultant

Baha Hanbala specializes in exhibition, museum and event design and execution consulting in Saudi Arabia, with over 6 years of field experience and involvement in 50+ projects for government and private-sector clients.

Among the projects he has contributed to is the Holy Quran Museum, the largest project of his career at over 3,000 m², alongside stands for real estate, medical and commercial companies at major exhibitions such as Cityscape, Seredo and Jewels of the World.
