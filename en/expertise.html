FAQ

Answers Before You Ask

How much does an exhibition stand cost?

Pricing depends on size, design complexity, materials and venue. Send your stand dimensions and exhibition name via the contact page for a detailed reply.

What materials are used?

Materials are chosen based on design and budget, typically including wood, aluminum, glass, acrylic, LED lighting and high-resolution printing.

How long does execution take?

It depends on size and design. Example: a 12×20 m stand at Seredo Jeddah 2026 was delivered within one week.

Which cities do you work in?

My base is Riyadh, with project involvement in Riyadh, Jeddah, the Eastern Province (Dammam and Dhahran) and Mulham.
