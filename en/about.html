About

Baha Hanbala

Exhibition, museum & event design and execution consultant based in Riyadh.

Baha Hanbala specializes in exhibition, museum and event design and execution consulting in Saudi Arabia, with over 6 years of field experience and involvement in more than 50 projects for government and private-sector clients.

Throughout his career, Baha has moved across several roles in exhibition and event project follow-up: from initial briefing and understanding client requirements, through monitoring design and fabrication stages, to on-site supervision of installation and handover. This hands-on path has given him a precise understanding of every stage, and the ability to confidently guide clients through it.

Among the projects he has contributed to is the Holy Quran Museum, the largest project of his career at over 3,000 m², alongside stands for real estate, medical and commercial companies at major exhibitions such as Cityscape, Seredo and Jewels of the World, and various events and festivals across Riyadh, Jeddah, the Eastern Province and Mulham.

Baha believes that the success of any exhibition project starts with uninterrupted, personal follow-through — from the first meeting to the moment the stand welcomes its first visitor.
